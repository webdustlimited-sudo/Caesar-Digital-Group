Good to know

What affects a website or app project?

Content

Text, photos, logos, product information and calls-to-action influence the quality and clarity of the final result.

Features

Forms, payments, bookings, dashboards, accounts, integrations and other functions add different levels of complexity.

Hosting & domain

A website needs a place to live and a domain name people can use to reach it. These are separate from design and development.

Maintenance

Websites and apps are not “build once and forget” products. Updates, security and improvements keep them useful over time.
