FAQ

Questions businesses often ask.

Do I need a website if I already use social media?

Social media is useful for discovery and engagement, but a website gives you a dedicated home for your business information, services, contact details and customer journey.

Can a website be updated after launch?

Yes. Content, pages, functionality and design can be improved over time. Maintenance makes those changes easier and helps keep the site healthy.

When should a business consider an app?

Consider an app when customers or staff repeatedly need to log in, submit information, track something, book, order, manage records or complete a workflow.

What do I need to provide for a website?

Useful starting material includes your logo, business information, services or products, contact details, photos and examples of websites you like. We can help structure the content and identify gaps.
