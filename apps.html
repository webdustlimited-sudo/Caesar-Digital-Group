Apps & business systems

Turn repetitive work into digital workflows.

A well-planned app can make it easier for customers to interact with your business and easier for teams to manage everyday processes.

Where apps help

Examples of useful business app ideas.

✓

Booking systems

Let customers request appointments, choose services and receive relevant information without long back-and-forth conversations.

◉

Customer portals

Give customers a secure place to view information, submit requests, track progress or access services.

▦

Internal dashboards

Centralise business information so staff can see tasks, records, reports or operational status in one place.

↗

E-commerce & ordering

Support product discovery, orders and customer journeys with a digital storefront or tailored ordering system.

⇄

Workflow automation

Connect steps in a process so information moves more consistently between people, forms and business systems.

＋

Custom business tools

If your process does not fit an off-the-shelf product, a custom web application can be designed around the actual workflow.

Website vs app

They solve different problems.

A website is often about being discovered, explaining what you do and generating enquiries. An app is often about interaction, accounts, workflows, transactions or repeated use. Some businesses benefit from both.

Plan an app →
1
Start with the problem

Define what should become easier, faster or more organised.

2
Map the users

Understand who uses the system and what each person needs to accomplish.

3
Design the simplest useful flow

A focused first version is usually easier to test and improve.

4
Maintain and improve

Real users reveal what should be refined after launch.
