Website types

Choose the right kind of website.

Different businesses need different online experiences. Here are common website directions we can design and build without locking you into a one-size-fits-all template.

▤

Business / company website

Present your company, services, team, location, story and contact options in a professional structure.

Best for: SMEs, agencies, firms
▥

Portfolio website

Showcase work, projects, case studies, skills, testimonials and a clear way for potential clients to enquire.

Best for: Creatives, professionals
◫

E-commerce website

Display products, categories and buying journeys, with the right checkout and payment approach for the business.

Best for: Product sellers
▦

Booking / appointment site

Help customers understand your services and request or schedule appointments through a streamlined experience.

Best for: Service businesses
▣

Landing page

A focused page built around one offer, campaign or action such as an enquiry, registration or product launch.

Best for: Campaigns & promotions
⌁

News / blog / content site

Publish articles, updates, announcements or educational content and build an audience around your expertise.

Best for: Publishers & organisations
What makes a good website?

Useful, fast and easy to navigate.

✓
Mobile-first experience

Most visitors may use a phone, so the layout should work beautifully on small screens.

✓
Clear calls-to-action

Visitors should know what to do next — contact, book, buy, enquire or learn more.

✓
Search-friendly structure

Good headings, useful content, performance and technical foundations help search visibility.

✓
Trust signals

Accurate information, testimonials, contact details and a professional presentation reduce uncertainty.
